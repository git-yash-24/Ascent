# Gate 1 (M4): feel test decision

Date: 2026-10-12

## Decision

Low-poly 3D is the style for the MVP (gate 2 daily beta and the world v1 in M9).

Xander's words: "I like the 3D one for its feel, but what we ultimately want to achieve is a
storytelling potential like Pine: A Story of Loss. The 3D version is OK for now since we are
working on the MVP."

So the decision is provisional on purpose. 3D carries the beta, and the storytelling target
is checked again at gate 3 (story slice), where the art direction can still move.

## Phone profile

Device from M1: Adreno 750, 411 × 781 CSS px at DPR 2.625, 8 cores, 8 GB.
The feel test's fps reading on the phone was not reported; it is to be recorded at M9 with the
performance probe.

## What the references ask of Ascent

| In the reference | What it does | Where it lands in Ascent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each tap or swipe is a physical chore: log on block, axe swing, barrow | The input is a ritual, not a form | Habit scene categories (prepare, repair, effort, care...) map each habit to one of Ari's chores; the micro tier shows the gesture |
| Wide shots cut to tight inserts of hands, axe, log | Intimacy; the act is the story | Short-tier habit scenes: one insert shot (glove, strap, flame) before returning wide |
| Translucent doubles of him repeating the same action (1:45, 2:42) | Repetition and time made visible | "Echoes": faint past selves at camp, built from real logged days; candidate for the week transition |
| A red bird against teal pines | One warm accent in a cold world | Palette rule: cold blue world, one warm figure (red parka), one warm fire |
| The image breaks into paint when the wife appears (2:57) | Memory as a different texture | Memory tier: painterly or sepia treatment, 2 to 3 s, at record moments |
| He stoops and looks down; no text | Grief through bearing | Bearing layer (resolve and fatigue) carries mood; no captions |
| Opens on a black star field, then the forest | Scale before intimacy | Daily opening and the sky level share the star field |

## Art direction changes adopted for M9

- Ari's outer layer becomes a red parka with a light hood trim (from the ChatGPT still); the
  world palette stays cold blue at dusk and night.
- Snow ground and faceted rocks around camp, with sparks drifting up from the fire.
- One insert shot per habit scene, from M13.
- Echo doubles to be prototyped in M9 as an option for the week view.
